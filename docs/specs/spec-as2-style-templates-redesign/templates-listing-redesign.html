How AlignSynch works

A shared starting point for two perspectives.

Each person answers the same Aspects privately. After both answer and accept, you can open a shared report and decide what to discuss next.

  1. 01
    Choose your relationship

    Start with a template that fits the two of you.

  2. 02
    Answer privately

    Both people respond to the same guided prompts.

  3. 03
    Review together

    See where expectations meet and what may need a conversation.
