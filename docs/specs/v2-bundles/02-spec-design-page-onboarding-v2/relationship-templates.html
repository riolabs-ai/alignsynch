How It Works

Four steps. Most people finish their first round in under 10 minutes.

  1. Step 1

    Pick a Template

    Start from one made for siblings, partners, co-founders and more.

  2. Step 2

    Invite One Person

    Set it up in about 4 minutes. Your 7-day free trial starts, no card.

  3. Step 3

    Answer Privately

    You each answer on the same 1–5 scale. Nobody sees answers until you both finish.

  4. Step 4

    See Where You Agree

    In Synch, Small Gap or Gap for every Aspect, plus one Next Step.

Questions

Is it really free to start?

Yes. Every relationship starts with a 7-day free trial and no card. Add a card only if you want to keep going.

Does the person I invite pay?

Never. The person who starts the relationship covers it. The person invited never sees a price.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you haven’t added a card, Stage 6 pauses. Everything is saved, and you get one free extra week if you need it.

Who sees my answers?

Nobody, until you both finish. Then you see each other’s answers side by side.

Can I change a template?

Yes. Turn Aspects off or reword them while you set up, or save your own copy.
