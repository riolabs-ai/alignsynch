CRUD & Permissions

The spec reference for every create, edit, archive and leave action. Read-only. Counts come from the matrix source.

Rules Every #1119 Child Must Honour

  • The Counterparty never sees a price, the word trial, a card or an extension.
  • Once Stage 6 is live, Aspect changes are proposed and agreed by both parties. Nothing changes under a party until both agree, and any answers that would be discarded are named first.
  • Archive over delete: templates archive or restore; permanent delete only when unused and unpublished; relationships close or are abandoned, never deleted.
  • The 1–5 answer scale is locked: never a field, never editable.
  • No silent edits. The Initiator or staff rewrite-with-confirm in lib/relationship/edit.ts is the conflict to replace, not to port. session.mode and session.alignment_setting stay immutable once the session exists (V18).
  • Destructive actions are graphite with an × icon, never red.

Conflicts and Decisions

  • Live Aspect edits

    Prototype
    Locked after Stage 5 (lock note was cut in pass 4, so the rule is invisible). Suggest-a-Change only at Accept.
    Upstream
    Initiator/staff may edit with stated-cost confirmation (`lib/relationship/edit.ts`). Counterparty has no write. New Aspect after answers is blocked (`answers-sealed`); new round is the path.
    v2 Rule
    Once Stage 6 is live, changes are proposed and agreed by both, never silent.
    Recommendation
    **Do not port silent edit.** Add Propose a Change. Restore a visible lock/propose note on the Aspects tab.

  • Mode / Alignment Tolerance after publish

    Prototype
    Settings tab lets the Initiator “Save Settings” (“applies from the next round”). Align already has a Soft/Full *compare* toggle.
    Upstream
    `session.mode` and `session.alignment_setting` are immutable once the session exists (V18).
    v2 Rule
    Align compare-toggle is fine. Persistent changes belong on a later round.
    Recommendation
    Keep the Align toggle. Disable or retarget Settings Save so it cannot rewrite a scored session.

  • Propose to Library

    Prototype
    Full form on `../06-spec-design-page-templates-v2/template-proposal.html`, mock submit.
    Upstream
    Members cannot publish. Catalogue is admin-curated (#533). No proposal queue.
    v2 Rule
    Undecided in the repo.
    Recommendation
    **Decision needed.** Debut can ship without member proposals (P1). Do not build a review queue unless Jaden wants it.

  • Settings “from the next round” vs a new round

    Prototype
    Copy implies a later round will pick up Mode/Tolerance.
    Upstream
    No “next round” writer is wired as a first-class member card in the surfaces reviewed; a new Aspect on an answered sheet is refused.
    v2 Rule
    New questions need a new round, agreed.
    Recommendation
    Treat “next round” as P1 product work, not a hidden Settings save.
