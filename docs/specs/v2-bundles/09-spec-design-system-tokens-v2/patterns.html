Shared Patterns: Modal, Toast, Kebab

Pass 10 uses exactly one of each. Every destructive confirm and small form is the neutral modal; every confirmation is the toast; every card menu is the kebab. No red, ever.

One Neutral Modal

components/ui/alert-dialog on a native dialog. Graphite confirm with an ×, ghost Cancel, focus returned on close.

One Toast

components/ui/toast: a single polite live region, optional link such as View or Undo.

One Kebab

components/ui/dropdown-menu: ghost icon trigger, role=menu, arrows, Escape and outside click close.
