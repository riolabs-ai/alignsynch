Brother–Sister

For a brother and sister who want to stay close as adults, see where they agree and talk about the rest.

Personal · Home5 AspectsAbout 3 Minutes2 People

What It Covers

Three themes, five Aspects.

  • How You Stay in Touch

    How often you talk, and how often each of you wants to.

  • Old Roles

    The roles from growing up that still shape how you treat each other.

  • Family Decisions

    Showing up for each other and deciding about your parents together.

The 5 Aspects

Every Aspect uses the same 1–5 scale. You can turn any off or reword it when you create.

Each of you answers every Aspect: Feel · How It Is NowWish · How You Want It Advanced Mode adds Think and Guess.

Who It’s For

  • Adult siblings living in different cities or seasons of life
  • Siblings sharing care or decisions for their parents
  • Anyone who wants to talk about old roles without a fight

Not for crisis support. If someone is unsafe, reach out to local help first.
