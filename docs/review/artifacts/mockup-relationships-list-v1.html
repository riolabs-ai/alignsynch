The card itselfunchanged — gray-50 header block, tint/soft/neutral chips, the seat chip, the Aspects and invitation chips, the primary action. The only edit is the footer row that now carries View + the menu.
  • Every control that exists today still exists. Open Assessment (disabled with its reason when the relationship has no session), Publish Assessment / Complete Relationship / Open Relationship — the primary keeps its verb-per-state rule — and View Relationship.
  • Two tabs carry a word for what they filter. running is stored active; the label comes from data/pages/dashboard-relationships.ts, so the tab and the card cannot disagree.
  • What I did not draw: Archive / Delete (no delete path exists — it is #743), a paid/Pay-now control (the card deliberately draws none, #739), and a sort menu (the read has no sort).
  • Two relationships from one template are the first two cards — the title is the relationship's own, which is what #850 landed.
